GNNPCSAFT Chat

In this app, you can chat with LLMs (Gemini or Ollama) using GNNPCSAFT tools, allowing you to retrieve PC-SAFT parameters for various compounds, predict thermodynamic properties, and gain insights into GNNPCSAFT performance.

Open Source AI-Powered Cross-Platform

How does it work?
Simply select the GNNPCSAFT tools to make them available to the LLM. When prompted, the LLM will use GNNPCSAFT to estimate pure-component PC-SAFT parameters for your molecules and calculate thermodynamic properties using the EoS.

You can validate the estimated parameters against properties such as density, vapor pressure, enthalpy of vaporization, phase diagrams, VLE, LLE, etc.

Tip: Find molecular identifiers at PubChem or build your own at DIYmol (right-click the 2D panel to copy SMILES).
